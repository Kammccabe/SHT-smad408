Our story

In 2000, Anthony Andriola became the sole owner of The Spring House Tavern. With over 30 years of passion and experience, Anthony will continue to uphold the custom of excellence while incorporating his vision to renew the vibrancy and tradition the Tavern has held for so many years. As you enjoy your experience at The Spring House Tavern, we invite you to share in our pride of decades of history and the thousands of guests who have passed through our doors.
